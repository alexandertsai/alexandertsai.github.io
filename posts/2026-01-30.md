Today my boss asked me if I knew of the phrase "生子当如孙仲谋". If you are not familiar, this phrase references Cao Cao (a ruthless but brilliant leader from around 1800 years ago), who upon meeting Sun Zhong Mo and seeing his large army, is impressed, and says the phrase (and a bit more), meaning that if you have a son, they should be impressive like Sun Zhong Mo. My boss said this while referencing me. I did not know the meaning of the phrase then, but my colleague explained she was praising me. 

I am guessing the reason for this comment was because when asked about my weekend plans, I mentioned that I am going to Stanford for some classes. I explained these classes are more targetted at adult learners but I wanted to attend anyway because I just wanted to experience something like this at least once in my life and I also think it'll be a great chance to meet new people (yes I had to pay quite a bit for them sadly...). While I don't feel that extraordinary, it was incredibly touching to hear that regardless. I've received a few compliments in my life but I think this up on the list with some of the sweetest.

And then later on I also got to show my colleagues my split keyboard and go to explain to them how it worked so that was really fun!
