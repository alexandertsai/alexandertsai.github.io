Yesterday I was talking to an old colleague of mine who had recently left the team. I had finished my internship on December 19th, and she had left our team on the 31st. She didn't leave the company though — just transferred to another department. Anyway, we were just texting and catching up about all sorts of things. I told her I was kinda sad I didn't get to hear her goodbye speech. She said that that was fine since I could still hear the one most important sentence. I was like OH tell me tell me. Okay ready to hear it??

"Maybe everybody has their own path to go, and I feel so grateful to have walked a part of mine with you."

Wowowow when I heard that I got hit HEAVY. Possibly one of the most, no the most, touching and sweet phrases I've heard. I asked her where she got that from and she told me she had just thought of it on the spot. For context, English isn't even her first language which to me just makes this hit even harder. I thought this was so sweet and I kid you not when I say I nearly started bawling in the office when hearing this.

People come and people go and they really do so at a rate sometimes so fast that it's unsettling. Not in an eerie, disturbing way, but quite literally doesn't allow you to settle. It's akin to the feeling of walking for ages around in a foreign country, reaching your destination, feeling that mix of relief and fatigue as you sink into your seat, only for your mom to tell you a minute later that you guys have got to go catch your train. Something like that...

This sentence really made me think about a lot of the wonderful people I've had the chance to not just work, but experience life with. A lot of things are so temporal like that and it's really gone before you know it. Something I used to and still do sometimes is to take a second to zone out. I find it forces me to treasue the moment I'm in. You know how staring at the clock and just wishing for time to pass faster makes it pass slower somehow (?) The method is similar to that in some ways.

Anyway concluding I told her I'm stealing this line and I'm going to use it the next time I depart!
