I enjoyed myself today. My team went out for team bonding. We went on the luge! Some people might think the luge is a pretty kiddie thing, but it ended up being a good amount of fun. Each ride was quite short, but I think having company and other people to talk to between the rides was what made the experience really special. We then had dinner at pretty fancy restaurant where we ordered a bunch of fragrant dishes, most of the names of which I've forgotten. 

![View from Cable Car](/images/luge_view.webp)

I ended up having to leave early because I had agreed to go for a spin class at CRNK with a couple of other friends. The class was not too bad, but I thought that the studio itself wasn't very well-designed. For one, the bicycles were all facing forward, so if you were sitting at the side it was harder to follow along because you'd have to raise your head and turn it towards the instructor. The instructor was also at a higher elevation that us, so if he started doing voodoo hand movements it was hard to see. Other than that it was not too bad. His "speciality" is that he would increase the BPM of the song during the song itself. So you'd start accelerating mid-song, which was quite a unique experience. I don't think I've ever spun that fast in my life.

I think a really important aspect of spin is the social aspect. A lot of things become a lot more fun the moment you have people to do those things with. Maybe spin in particular provides a great medium for just the right amount of challenge and bonding.
