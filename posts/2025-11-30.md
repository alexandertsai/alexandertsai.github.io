I was reading through some of my old posts on Tumblr when I stumbled upon this one. I'll paste it here for ease of reference:

>I believe one of the most ethereal things people can create are memories. It’s odd because the memory you create is at its most ethereal form only when it’s presented to you. It’s a personal gift; from you to yourself. People can choose to amplify and remember certain parts of an event better than other parts, thereby allowing a personalised remembrance.

>Usually things aren’t exactly as people remember them to be. But people still have no issue discussing the areas in which they do overlap, and fondly laughing about events that have occurred before. Isn’t that quite wondrous? You can laugh once during a funny event, and laugh a couple more times just talking about that event.

>Sometimes we have bad memories. Most people would say forgive and forget or forgive and don’t forget or don’t forget nor forgive - whatever it is, we have them. I believe in forgiving. We don’t need to forget certain things happen but we shouldn’t grasp onto them too tightly.

>Anyway, there is a point to this post. I wrote this to say that if you’re reading this and an old friend, or those mornings where your mother would send you to school, or a place you used to like to walk at come to mind - these aren’t just memories. Reconnect. Give a close family member a call. Talk to the people who you treasure deeply and make as many memories with them as you can before you can’t anymore. Isn’t that one of the best parts of living?

I realised this post has 4 likes on Tumblr. Amazing. For context, most of my posts have 0 likes. Anyway I wanted to revisit this because I thought it was something very very sweet. Especially on the part about memories. It is true in a lot of ways that we (consciously or unconsciously) often choose a certain way to remember an event. Some people remember it fondly, others not so much. Anyway, the focus of this post wasn't really on how we view memories but rather just the act of reminiscing upon them. I notice there are times where I might feel unhappy with a certain situation or person but upon remembering some of the good aspects of the situation in the past or the good things that have happened with that person, you gain a much more objective view. You might think ah I really suck at something or this person hates me... but then you've just gotta remember hey I accomplished xyz before or this person said yyzzxx to me.

Humans are inherently biased towards recalling negative events in their life. Maybe then the only way to lead a somewhat balanced life is to be positively biased! To deliberately think about positive things. And well I suppose if you've thought of all the positive things and the situation is still in a negative light then it really must be bad sorry.
