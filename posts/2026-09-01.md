Anyway the point of this post isn't really about Shabu Sai, even though I think it's not very good and I don't think it's worth the money. This post is about my New Year's Resolution! (NYR)

To be clear, I don't usually do any resolutions but this year's NYR wasn't so much as an NYR but rather something I wanted to do in general. It just so happened that this one fell into the period in which New Years came along. Onto the goal: this year I'm focused on making optimal decisions by the hour.

Let's say it's 8:55am and you catch yourself scrolling on Instagram. At 9:00am (the next hour), you should attempt to evaluate that decision and do the thing that you know will bring you the greatest amount of joy. For reference, when discussing quantities of joy, we are referring to joy received now plus joy received later on in life. Since joy now is worth more than joy later (temporal discounting), we can assign some discount factor Gamma (0.9 or so) to future joy values. 

For example, we might decide scrolling Instagram brings 5 joy now (assuming joy is out of 10 and that the maximum possible joy is 10) but brings absolutely 0 joy later (we don't spend time reminiscing on that funny reel we scrolled across). Then we can say that the net joy brought about by scrolling Instagram now is 5. However, if I instead decided to exercise now, the immediate joy I might gain might only be 3 (exercise isn't really fun, but there is probably some serotonin released during the process), but we can consider the future joy in the afternoon (maybe 2 or 3 - the satisfaction from exercising) and also the joy in the days after (the happiness brought along by generally being fit and having a good body) actually ends up pushing exercise slightly over doomscrolling in terms of net joy.

This general framework can be applied to almost every decision made in life by every hour (including writing this blog post). Hence, my goal this year will be to make optimal joy decisions by the hour!
