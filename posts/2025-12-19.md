On my second last day of my internship, a few of us went out for a team lunch at Waa Cow. On the way there, Zhiliang and I talked about San Jose. He told me that people there taxi everywhere and there really wasn't such a thing as public transport, but that the weather was great and everyone was pretty friendly. When we got there, I ordered a bowl of Chirashi and suggested Truffle fries to share. Jessica tried to get the bill but everyone suggested they split evenly.

At around 4 we had my final team meeting. My section of the team meetings were funny because there would be a section like 下周计划, and I'd write 暂时没有。At the end of the meeting, Jessica asked if I wanted to give a small goodbye speech of sorts. I told the team I really appreciated the fact that they provided such a supportive environment and that working with them was a joy. On Lark, people can react with certain emojis or expression to indicate "OK" or a thumbs up when someone says something (similar to Zoom emojis). As I said that, I got many heart reactions, which made me really touched :')

On my last day, I gave out cookies which I baked that morning. I tried to use dark chocolate as the chips because I remember Zhiliang saying 不喜欢太甜的。I returned my laptop at 3, showered at 5, and left the office at 5:30 for the airport.

There's a Chinese phrase known as "难以言喻", meaning something that is hard to describe or put into words. This internship gave me a lot of valuable learning opportunities and allowed me to meet some truly wonderful people. I don't think there was ever a singular moment wherein I felt dissatisfied or regretful. I'll miss the lunches, the team bonding sessions, escaping the office for walks around Marina, talking to Brandon about my strategies, sitting around unproductively with Ashley, the pantry, the whiteboards, the occasional coffee runs, and most of all the people. This internship was everything I could ask for and more. The feelings of gratitude, longing and bittersweetness is really 难以言喻。
