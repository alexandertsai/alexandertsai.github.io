### On Excellence And Things I'm Not

I'm not sure why, but ever since I was a little child I could never be the best. I know it's asking for a LOT to be the best but I seriously think everyone once in a while should at least top the class in something right? Like even if it's just once or twice in their life. I've never managed to do that. No matter how hard I work, no matter how much I pour everything I have into some tiny area, I can never be the best. Worst thing is I'm not even 2nd. Maybe like 4th or something. And it's strange because at certain points in my life I have been fairly hardworking, maybe even the hardest working, but I could never quite get it.

It bothers me sometimes. Especially when it comes to school work. I don't think I've gotten full marks on anything ever since coming to SMU. The closest I've got was maybe a 97. But I can never quite get full marks. And it really bothers me. There are things I put so much time and effort into, but it never seems to amount to full marks. There's always some careless mistake somewhere or something that goes wrong. I can't ever achieve excellence. Sometimes I wonder if NUS would have been better. But I think I would have gotten crushed there. I'd say there are a fair number of students there that are quite excellent.

Anyway! Not trying to be a negative nelly here. I'm just going to keep trying my best. Keep figuring out what is going wrong and prevent that same mistake from happening in the future. It's the same thing when building a model. You test it and iterate upon it again and again and again until it becomes good. This is just like data science but with myself as the dataset!
