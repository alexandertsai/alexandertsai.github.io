Time is precious. It is one of the few things that cannot be increased. Almost everything else - emotional fulfilment, money, health (to some extent), relationships - can fluctuate based on our actions. We cannot ever increase the amount of time we have left.

What concerns me is the amount of time people spend on social media today. In particular, I am a heavy critique of short-form videos. I don't need to cite any studies besides your screentime to demonstrate the enormous amount of time spent on these apps. 

What I find strange is that a lot of us are acutely aware of this, and many even acknowledge it may be a problem, but no one seems to be doing anything. Worst, the ones who perform a "social media detox", often film the process and upload them onto those very platforms themselves. 

There are many reasons people scroll on their phones. Most of these stem from boredom, habit, or addiction. I often hear people say they need a day to doomscroll. Some people view it as temporary respite from stressful lives, akin to going to a spa. Although, we should be honest. Most people do it far more than needed.
