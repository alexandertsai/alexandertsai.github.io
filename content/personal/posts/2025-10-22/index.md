We were trying to derive a strategy to bid for a class that previously had two sections but now only had one. We ended up taking the weighted average of their minimum bid amounts (weighted by the number of people within each class, 24/45 and 38/45 respectively), and then assuming there was a linear relationship between the number of people who wanted to bid for a class and the bid price, and that the number of people who want to bid for a class remains constant (there would be 52 people bidding for 45 slots), we can then multiply the weighted average of that minimum price against the ratio of the number of people who want to bid for a class over its capacity (52/45). We ended up with a price of around 23 ecredits. So I suppose we are bidding that.
