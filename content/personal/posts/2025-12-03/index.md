It's a safe assumption that most people do not enjoy the feeling of regret. There are times when you feel like you might regret something later (like lazing around with an exam the next day) but you do that thing anyway. I really wonder why we do that. My guess is that somehow our previous feelings of regret have been given time to be processed and we slip back into the same patterns. It's a sort of negative effect of things healing with time.

I think the next thing I want to create is something like a past self speaking to you. There are a lot of lessons that would be learned a lot more easily if I could just speak to the me back then that had felt the pain from those lessons so intensely. Funnily enough I write this while I have an exam tomorrow... I'm just hoping that I'm able to do things that I don't regret.
